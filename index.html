TEST CHECKLIST — DO THESE ON THE PHONE

  • Open a film in portrait: does the scene fill the screen (no tiny letterboxed strip)?
  • Let it play ~60 s: does the camera drift smoothly between phases, or jump/cut?
  • Are the labels and captions readable? Does the caption clear the transport deck (not hide behind it)?
  • Tap the canvas to pause / resume. Tap the big play button.
  • Drag the scrubber left and right — does it track your finger without page zoom/scroll kicking in?
  • Tap each numbered phase dot along the top — all of them reachable (λ has 8)?
  • Rotate to landscape mid-playback, then back to portrait — does everything reposition correctly?
  • Try 0.5× / 1× / 2× speed and the loop toggle.
  • Turn sound on/off (speaker icon) — does it work without a visible error?
  • Run one full film: any lag, stuttering, or noticeable battery/heat?
  • If you can: repeat in both iOS Safari and Android Chrome.
